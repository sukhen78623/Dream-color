Free duotone image generator

A duotone maps the dark parts of a photo to one color and the light parts to another, so the whole picture is built from just two hues and the tones between them. It is the look behind many album covers, posters and website hero images, because it makes any photo match your brand colors.

This tool converts your image to light and dark values, then paints them with a smooth OKLab color ramp. You can add a third midtone color for a tritone, match a shadow to your highlight with color harmony, or let the tool pick a pair from your own photo. Export a PNG, or copy an SVG filter or CSS to apply the same effect live on your website.

Frequently asked questions

What is a duotone image?

A duotone is an image printed or shown with two colors. Dark areas take the shadow color, bright areas take the highlight color, and everything in between blends smoothly from one to the other.

How do I make a duotone photo online?

Upload or drop your photo, choose a shadow and a highlight color or tap a preset, then adjust contrast and intensity. When you like the result, download it as a PNG or JPG.

Which colors work best for a duotone?

Pick a dark shadow and a clearly lighter highlight. A contrast ratio of 4.5 or more usually keeps details readable, and the tool shows the ratio for your pair. The Auto from image button suggests colors based on your photo.

What is a tritone and what does the midtone do?

A tritone adds a third color between the shadow and the highlight. It gives the image more depth and keeps midtones from looking flat. Turn on the midtone option to try it.

How do I add a duotone effect with CSS?

Copy the SVG filter from the export section, add it once to your page, then apply filter: url(#duotone) to any image. The CSS blend option is a lighter alternative that works best when the shadow is darker than the highlight.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is processed entirely in your browser, so it never leaves your device.
