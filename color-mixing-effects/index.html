Frequently asked questions

How do I mix two colors?

Pick both colors in the Gradient Generator & Color Mixer, set the ratio, and the mixed color appears with its HEX, RGB and HSL values.

What is the difference between a tint, a shade and a tone?

A tint is a color mixed with white, a shade is a color mixed with black, and a tone is a color mixed with gray. Tints and shades are handy for UI color scales.

How do I get the CSS code for a gradient?

Choose your colors, angle and stops in the Gradient Generator & Color Mixer and copy the generated CSS.

What is a duotone?

A duotone maps an image or background to two colors, one for the dark areas and one for the light areas, for a bold, graphic look.
