Frequently asked questions

What contrast ratio does WCAG require?

WCAG AA asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and UI components. AAA asks for 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

What is color blindness?

Color blindness, or color vision deficiency, is a reduced ability to tell certain colors apart. It is most common in men, so it is worth checking that your design does not rely on color alone.

Do colors mean the same thing everywhere?

No. Color meanings vary by culture and context, so treat them as common associations rather than fixed rules.

How do I make an accessible color palette?

Pick colors, then test each text and background pair in the Contrast & WCAG Checker. The Palette Generator also has an accessible option that keeps pairs readable.
