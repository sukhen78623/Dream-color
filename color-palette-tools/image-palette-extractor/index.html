How it works


Extract a palette in three steps


1. Add an image
Choose a photo, drag it onto the box, or paste it. You can also try the sample image.
2. See its colors
The tool finds the main colors and shows how much of the image each one covers.
3. Copy or download
Tap a color to copy its HEX code, or export the palette as a list, CSS variables, JSON or a PNG.







Questions


Image Palette Extractor FAQ


How does the extractor find the colors?
It shrinks the image and groups similar pixels together. The center of each group becomes a palette color, and the groups are ordered by how much of the image they cover.
Is my image uploaded?
No. Your image is read by your browser and never sent to a server.
How many colors can I extract?
From 3 to 10. Use fewer colors for a simple palette and more colors for detailed photos.
Why are the colors slightly different from what I see?
The palette shows the average of each color group, so it can look a little softer than a single pixel. Use the Image Color Picker when you need an exact pixel.
Which image types work?
Any image your browser can show, such as PNG, JPG, WebP and GIF.
