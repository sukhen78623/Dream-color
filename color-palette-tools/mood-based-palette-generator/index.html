Questions

Mood-based Palette Generator FAQ

How does a mood turn into colors?

Each mood has a range of hues, saturation and lightness that people often link with that feeling. The tool picks colors inside that range, so every idea fits the mood but looks different.

Will the same mood always give the same palette?

No. Each click creates new colors inside the same mood, so you can keep generating until you find one you like.

Do colors mean the same thing to everyone?

Not always. Color feelings depend on culture, context and personal experience, so treat moods as a helpful starting point, not a rule.

Can I share a mood with someone?

Yes. The address of the page updates with the mood you chose, so a copied link opens the same mood.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The palettes are made in your browser.
