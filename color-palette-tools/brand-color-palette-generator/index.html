Questions


Brand Color Palette Generator FAQ


What do primary, secondary and accent colors do?
The primary color is the face of your brand, used for the logo, headers and main buttons. The secondary color supports it. The accent is used sparingly to draw attention, such as on calls to action. The background and text colors handle readability.
Why do I start from one color?
Most brands already have one color they love, such as the one in their logo. The generator builds the rest of the system around it, so your main color is never changed.
What do the styles change?
Balanced picks a neighbouring secondary color and a complementary accent. Bold contrast uses colors spread further around the color wheel. Soft and friendly uses gentler, lighter colors.
What do the AA and AAA labels mean?
They show the contrast of the best text color on that background, based on the WCAG guidelines. AA is a good minimum for normal text, AAA is stronger, and Low means text may be hard to read.
Where do I paste the CSS variables?
Paste them into your main stylesheet, then use them like color: var(--brand-primary). Changing a value updates every place that uses it.
