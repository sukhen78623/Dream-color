Frequently asked questions

How do I make a color palette from one color?

Enter or pick a base color in the Palette Generator, choose a harmony rule, and you get a balanced five-color palette that you can fine-tune shade by shade.

What is a color harmony?

A color harmony is a set of colors that work well together because of where they sit on the color wheel. Common types are complementary, analogous, triadic and tetradic schemes.

Can I get a color palette for a website or a logo?

Yes. Choose the website, UI, logo, interior or social media use case and the generator adapts the palette to that job.

Is the color palette generator free?

Yes. Every tool on yourColorsss is free to use, and you can copy HEX codes without signing up.
