How it works

Find a palette in three steps

1. Browse

Scroll through 48 hand-picked palettes, or choose a category such as Warm, Pastel, Dark or Vintage.

2. Search or save

Type a name to search. Tap Save on the palettes you like and find them again under Saved.

3. Copy

Tap any color to copy its HEX code, or tap Copy to take all five colors at once.

Questions

Palette Explorer FAQ

How are the palettes chosen?

They are hand-picked sets of five colors that work well together, grouped by look and feel, so you can browse by style instead of starting from scratch.

Where are my saved palettes stored?

Saved palettes are stored in your browser on this device. They are not uploaded, and they can be cleared if you clear your browser data.

Can I use these palettes in my projects?

Yes. Colors cannot be owned, so you can use them in your own designs, websites and documents.

How do I copy a single color?

Tap the color block. Its HEX code is copied and a short message confirms it.

Can I change a palette I like?

Yes. Copy the colors and open them in the Color Harmony Generator or Color Palette Generator to adjust them.
