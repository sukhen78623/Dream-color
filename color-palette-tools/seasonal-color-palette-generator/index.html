Questions

Seasonal Color Palette Generator FAQ

What makes a palette seasonal?

Seasonal palettes use the colors we link with that time of year, such as blossom pinks in spring, sea blues in summer, rust and amber in autumn, and icy blues and deep greens in winter.

What is the difference between the hand-picked and generated palettes?

Hand-picked palettes are fixed sets chosen by us. Generated ideas are created on the spot inside the season's color range, so they change every time.

Can I use these for fashion or home decor?

Yes. They work as inspiration for wardrobes, rooms, social posts and campaigns. Check swatches in real light before you buy paint or fabric.

Are these seasons the same everywhere?

These palettes follow the common four-season look. Seasons feel different in different regions, so use them as a starting point.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
