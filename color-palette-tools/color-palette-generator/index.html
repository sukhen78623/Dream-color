How it works


Build a palette in three steps




1. Generate
Choose how many colors you want and a style, then press Generate or the space bar.


2. Lock what you like
Tap the lock on any color to keep it. Generate again and only the other colors change.


3. Fine-tune and copy
Tap the pencil to adjust a color, then copy the HEX codes or CSS variables, or download a PNG.







Questions


Color Palette Generator FAQ




What do the style options do?
Style changes how saturated and light the colors are. Balanced is a mix, Pastel is soft and light, Vivid is bold, Dark is deep and moody, and Muted is calm and subtle.


How do I keep a color and change the rest?
Tap the lock icon on the color you want to keep. When you generate again, the new colors are built around the locked one.


Can I share or save my palette?
Yes. The page address updates with your colors, so you can bookmark it or send the link to someone and they will see the same palette.


Is my palette uploaded anywhere?
No. The generator runs in your browser, and nothing you create is sent to a server.
