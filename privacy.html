Privacy Policy

How YourColorsss (yourcolorsss.com) treats your information, in plain words.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version: You can use our color tools without creating an account. The colors and images you work with are handled in your browser, on your own device. We do not sell your data. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

1. Who we are

YourColorsss is a free website of color tools at yourcolorsss.com. It is run by Sukhendu Roy of Sukhendu Software Solutions, Howrah, West Bengal, India. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean YourColorsss and the people behind it.

2. Information we handle

  • Nothing required from you. You do not need to sign up or log in to use the tools.
  • Colors, palettes and images you use in the tools. These are processed in your browser on your device. We do not upload them to our servers and we do not store them.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the site.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is normal for any website and is used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Settings saved on your device. The site may save simple preferences, such as light or dark mode, in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, and you can clear it any time from your browser settings.

3. Cookies

We do not set our own tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this page first and say clearly what is collected.

4. Third-party services

  • Hosting and delivery. The site is delivered through Cloudflare. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download them, and Google may receive your IP address. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. How we use information

We use the little information we handle to run and secure the website, to answer your messages, and to fix bugs and improve the tools.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above so the site can work, or if the law requires us to.

7. How long we keep it

We keep emails only as long as needed to deal with your message and for reasonable record keeping. Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider under its own policy.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop using it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 may give you additional rights. You can also clear saved preferences any time from your browser settings.

9. Children

YourColorsss is a general tool and is not aimed at young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the site and the little information we handle. No website can promise perfect security, but we keep the amount of data we handle to a minimum on purpose.

11. Links to other sites

Our pages may link to other websites, for example Save Your Trade. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Write to sukhendu.roy@saveyourtrade.in.
