How colors can make people feel

Feels like:

Positive:
Excitement, courage, love, warmth
Negative:
Anger, danger, warning
Useful for:
sales banners, food brands, calls to action

#2563EB

Feels like:
Trust, stability, calm
Positive:
Reliability, focus, peace
Negative:
Sadness, coldness, distance
Useful for:
banks, software, healthcare

#16A34A

Feels like:
Growth, balance, nature
Positive:
Calm, health, freshness, renewal
Negative:
Envy, inexperience, stagnation
Useful for:
wellness, finance, sustainability

#EAB308

Feels like:
Cheer, attention, optimism
Positive:
Happiness, clarity, curiosity
Negative:
Caution, anxiety, eye strain in excess
Useful for:
highlights, signage, playful branding

#9333EA

Feels like:
Creativity, luxury, imagination
Positive:
Wisdom, ambition, spirituality
Negative:
Moodiness, extravagance
Useful for:
beauty, creative tools, premium brands

#EA580C

Feels like:
Enthusiasm, friendliness, creativity
Positive:
Optimism, playfulness, appetite
Negative:
Impulsiveness, restlessness
Useful for:
youth brands, food, buttons that invite action

Color meanings are not universal scientific rules. Reactions vary with culture, context and personal experience, so treat these as common associations, not guarantees.
