Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

A HEX code has three pairs of digits for red, green and blue. Convert each pair to a number from 0 to 255. For example, #7C3AED is rgb(124, 58, 237).

How do I convert RGB to HEX?

Convert each of the red, green and blue values to a two-digit hexadecimal number and join them. For example, rgb(79, 47, 224) is #4F2FE0.

What is the difference between HEX, RGB, HSL and CMYK?

HEX and RGB describe colors for screens, HSL describes hue, saturation and lightness in a way that is easier to adjust, and CMYK describes ink mixes for print.

How do I pick a color from an image?

Upload the image in the Image Picker & Extractor and click any point to get its HEX, RGB and HSL values.

Is the CMYK conversion exact?

Screen to CMYK is an approximation. Printed colors also depend on the paper, ink and printer profile.
