Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

Type or paste the HEX code, for example #3B82F6, into the color box. The RGB, HSL, HSV, CMYK, LAB and OKLCH values appear instantly and can be copied with one tap.

What is the difference between RGB and CMYK?

RGB mixes light and is used for screens. CMYK mixes ink and is used for print. The CMYK values here are a standard mathematical conversion, so real print results depend on the printer and paper.

What contrast ratio do I need for accessible text?

WCAG AA needs at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

What is OKLCH?

OKLCH is a modern CSS color format based on human vision. Lightness, chroma and hue stay predictable, so palettes and gradients look more even than with HSL.

How do I pick a color from an image?

Use the image color picker tool. Upload a photo, tap any spot, and send the exact color back here to convert it into every format.

Are my colors stored online?

No. Saved colors stay in your own browser on your device and are never sent anywhere.
