Free image color picker

An image color picker finds the exact color of any pixel in a photo, screenshot or logo. Upload an image, tap the spot you want, and get its HEX, RGB, HSL, CMYK and OKLCH codes ready to paste into CSS, Figma, Canva or Photoshop. A zoom circle follows your finger so you can choose a single pixel with confidence.

The palette extractor groups similar colors in your image and shows the main ones with their share of the picture. It is a quick way to build a color scheme from a product photo, a painting, a brand image or a landscape, then export it as CSS variables, JSON or a PNG strip.

How to get a color from an image

  1. Choose a photo, or drop or paste one.
  2. Tap or drag on the image and watch the zoom circle.
  3. Tap any code to copy it, or open the color in the Color Picker for more formats.
  4. Use the extracted palette to copy or download the main colors.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the HEX code of a color in an image?

Upload the image and tap the spot you want. The HEX code appears right away and you can copy it with one tap.

How does the palette extractor work?

It samples the pixels of your image and groups similar colors together. The center of each group becomes a palette color, and the groups are sorted by how much of the image they cover.

Can I pick a color from a screenshot or logo?

Yes. Any image your device can open works, including screenshots, logos, photos and scans. For the most accurate result use the original image rather than a heavily compressed copy.

Why does the color look different from what I see on screen?

Photos can include color profiles and compression, and displays show colors differently. The tool reads the pixel value exactly as your browser draws it.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is processed entirely in your browser, so it never leaves your device.

What can I do with the exported palette?

Copy CSS variables for your website, JSON for an app or design tool, a simple HEX list, or download a PNG strip to share with your team.
